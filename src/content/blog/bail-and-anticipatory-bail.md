# Understanding Bail and Anticipatory Bail

Bail and anticipatory bail are different remedies and the appropriate application depends on the facts and stage of a criminal matter. The exact legal position should always be assessed with current case details.

## Bail after arrest

Bail is generally considered after a person has been arrested or is in custody. The court may consider the allegations, investigation, evidence, and other circumstances prescribed by law.

## Anticipatory bail

Anticipatory bail is a request for protection from arrest when a person has reason to believe that they may be arrested for a non-bailable offence. It is a discretionary remedy and is not automatic.

Because deadlines and procedural requirements can be important, anyone facing a possible arrest should obtain prompt, case-specific legal advice.
