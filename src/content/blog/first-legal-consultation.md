# How to Prepare for Your First Legal Consultation

A first consultation is most useful when the advocate can quickly understand the facts, the documents, and the outcome you need. A little preparation can make the meeting more focused.

## Bring the essential documents

Bring notices, agreements, identity documents, previous orders, correspondence, and any other material directly related to the issue. Keep the originals safely stored and provide copies where possible.

## Prepare a short timeline

Write down the important events in date order. Include deadlines, notices received, hearings, payments, and any previous legal advice. A clear timeline helps identify urgent steps.

## Ask direct questions

Note the questions you want answered, including likely next steps, documents still needed, expected timelines, and possible costs. Complete and accurate information allows for better advice.
